# The Ancient Abyss

**The Ancient Abyss** is a large-scale Minecraft Bedrock add-on built around a new endgame dimension. It is designed to turn the Dragon Egg into the beginning of another adventure, with its own world, progression, equipment, creatures, encounters, and secrets.

## What we're building

The Ancient Abyss is intended to feel like a complete dimension rather than a small collection of new blocks and mobs.

- **A new endgame journey:** Discover a hidden portal in an Ancient City and unlock access to the Abyss through a progression path involving the Ender Dragon's Dragon Egg and rare Null Cores found in End Ships.
- **A distinct dimension:** Explore a dark, vast world inspired by the mystery of the deep ocean and the atmosphere of a fantasy abyss. Its identity includes unusual terrain, custom biomes, hanging ruins, underground spaces, caves, and a low enclosed ceiling.
- **New resources and progression:** Mine dimension-specific materials and develop a new equipment tier, including Abyssalite, with its own crafting and upgrade path.
- **Creatures and allies:** Encounter original hostile creatures and powerful enemies, while also discovering the Echo Dwellers and systems for interacting and trading with them. The project also includes a way to create a friendly Resonant Warden.
- **Survival systems:** Build and use dimension-specific mechanics for resting, respawning, protection, and surviving the Abyss.
- **Bosses and encounters:** Work through increasingly dangerous encounters, unique enemy abilities, a citadel, and a final confrontation with Herobrine.
- **A cohesive visual and audio identity:** Develop custom block and item textures, models, animations, sounds, lighting, fog, and effects so the dimension feels like one connected world.

These features are being developed and refined in stages. Some systems are already present in the project, while others remain part of the planned experience.

## Visual direction

The Abyss should have a strong visual identity of its own—not simply look like default Minecraft with darker fog. We want rich colours, striking environments, distinctive materials, and atmosphere. We also intend to explore compatibility with vibrant visual and resource packs, including packs such as Prizma, rather than making the experience depend only on vanilla visuals.

## Our ambition

Our goal is to push Minecraft Bedrock add-on development far beyond what players normally expect. We want **The Ancient Abyss** to rival or surpass at least 80% of Java Edition mods in overall ambition, depth, gameplay, and presentation—and give Java players a reason to be jealous. 💀

That is the standard we're aiming for, not a claim that the project has already reached it.

**The Ancient Abyss is a work in progress.** We are building it phase by phase, connecting its world generation, progression, creatures, combat, and presentation into one substantial Bedrock experience.
