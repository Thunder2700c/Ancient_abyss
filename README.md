# The Ancient Abyss

**The Ancient Abyss** is an ambitious Minecraft Bedrock add-on built around a custom dimension designed to feel like a complete, dangerous world of its own—not just a small collection of blocks and mobs.

## What we're building

We're expanding the Abyss into a place worth exploring and surviving. The project focuses on:

- **A distinct world:** custom biomes, terrain, underground cave systems, and an enclosed atmosphere.
- **Exploration and mining:** dimension-specific resources, ores, materials, and progression.
- **Creatures and encounters:** original mobs, challenging enemies, bosses, and their own mechanics.
- **Gameplay systems:** equipment, crafting and upgrades, interactions, and connected features that make exploration meaningful.
- **A cohesive experience:** custom textures, models, animations, sounds, and visual effects that bring the dimension together.

Everything is being developed with Minecraft Bedrock's add-on capabilities and limitations in mind. The goal is to make these systems work together as one substantial, polished experience.

## Our vision

We want to push Minecraft Bedrock add-ons much further than people expect. Our long-term ambition is to create a mod that can rival or surpass **at least 80% of Java Edition mods** in overall experience, depth, and polish—and give Java players a reason to be jealous. 💀

That is the ambition we're working toward, not a benchmark we've already claimed to have achieved.

**The Ancient Abyss is a work in progress.** The world, systems, and content are being built and refined phase by phase.
